# Tape Reel 07: Audio Session Archive

Inside the grandfather clock compartment sits a collection of magnetic reel tapes, logged under patient codes. Most reels have been wiped clean with demagnetizing pulses.

One damaged reel remains: **Recording Seven**.

According to {{victim.full}}'s session notes, this tape records a therapy session with {{mira.full}}, who has refused to speak out loud for three years following the tragedy of 2 December 2023.

In the audio recording, Dr. Vane speaks, but Mira does not utter a word. Instead, she taps rhythmically against the wooden armrest of her consultation armchair. The surrounding dialogue is heavily degraded by tape hiss, but her distinct sequence of taps survives.

**Your Objective:**
Decode the rhythmic tapping sequence in the audio player to recover what Mira was communicating.
