### Tier 1
Listen to the cadence of the taps. There are two distinct sound lengths: quick short taps and prolonged taps.

### Tier 2
Short taps correspond to dots (·) and longer taps correspond to dashes (—). Shorter pauses separate characters; longer intervals separate words.
