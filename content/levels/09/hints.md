### Tier 1
Carefully read the comment next to each variable. Blank 1 converts hours to total minutes. How many minutes are in one hour?

### Tier 2
The original manipulation shifted the timestamp backward by 42 minutes to create a fake alibi. To reverse this forgery and find the real time, you must add (`+`) the shift back.

### Tier 3
After determining the total minutes elapsed in the day, the remaining minutes beyond full hours are calculated using the modulo/remainder operator (`%`). Finally, evaluate the expression: (22 * 60 + 26) + 42 = 1388 minutes. Convert 1388 into hours and minutes.
